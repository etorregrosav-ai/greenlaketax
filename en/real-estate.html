Real estate investment

Taxation on buying, selling and renting property in Spain

We support you through every property transaction with clarity on taxes, fees and strategies to optimise your investment.

Buying a property Acquisition

Property Transfer Tax (ITP) 6–10%

Applies to: the purchase of second-hand properties. The exact rate depends on the region.

VAT (IVA) 10%

Applies to: new properties purchased directly from the developer.

Stamp Duty (AJD) 0.5–1.5%

Applies to: the purchase price, varying by region.

Notary and Land Registry fees

Notary: 0.5–1% of the purchase price.
Land Registry: 0.1–0.5% of the purchase price.

Selling a property Disposal

Capital Gains Tax 19–28%

Progressive rates applied to the gain made on the sale. Exemptions may apply for sellers over 65 whose primary residence qualifies.

Municipal Capital Gains Tax (Plusvalía)

Based on the cadastral value and the length of ownership; the amount varies by municipality.

Renting out a property Operation

Personal Income Tax (IRPF) 19–54%

Progressive rates depending on total income. Deductible expenses include mortgage interest and maintenance costs.

VAT (IVA) 21%

Not applicable to long-term residential rentals; applies to holiday rentals.

Non-resident withholding tax 24%

On gross rental income, applicable to non-resident property owners.

Recommendation: we recommend professional tax guidance to ensure compliance and optimise your tax liabilities in any property transaction in Spain.
